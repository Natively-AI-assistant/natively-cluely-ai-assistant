## Summary

Six weeks of work since v2.8.8: a sharper Auto Answer, Visual Answers, Phone Mirror 2.0, Calendar sync, embeddings and rerankers, and a refreshed look.

## What's New

- Auto Answer (Beta): now skips questions meant for someone else on the call, and answers sooner. Off by default; turn it on in Settings → General.
- Visual Answers: system designs, flows and charts, drawn live inside the answer. Just ask for one.
- Phone Mirror 2.0: the live transcript, streaming answers and photos from your phone. In Settings → Sync.
- Calendar sync: meetings link to their calendar event, with names and a follow-up email. In Settings → Calendar.
- Embeddings and rerankers: choose what powers search over your files and meetings, from Gemini, OpenAI, Voyage AI, Jina AI or local models. In Settings → Retrieval.
- Better UI and animations: Liquid Glass controls, a new startup splash, and smoother motion in every window.

## Improvements

- Answers stay closer to your material: a checking pass removes claims your own documents do not support.
- Coding answers scale with the question, so a quick ask gets a short answer.
- Provider errors are explained in plain words in the overlay instead of a raw error line.
- Speech-to-text responds faster on Deepgram, Soniox and OpenAI.

## Fixes

- Speech-to-text: Google no longer goes silent when Chinese is selected, Soniox recovers after an idle stretch, and ElevenLabs returns text at every pause.
- Overlay: clicks on the pill, the expand button and the model picker no longer get lost.
- Auto Answer: a prepared answer that was never shown now appears, and your own speech no longer silences the answers that follow.
- Shortcuts: a shortcut the system refuses no longer crashes the app, and Natively no longer takes Cmd/Ctrl+R and Shift+Arrow from other apps.
- Windows: the screenshot cropper no longer steals focus, and the extra taskbar buttons are gone.

## Also in this release

**More features**

- Reads images: every model gets an Auto, On or Off setting for screenshots. On Auto, Natively works out whether the model can read images and stops sending screenshots to one that cannot. In Settings → AI Providers.
- Direct Assist (Beta): sends what you typed, said or captured straight to the model, unprocessed, together with the meeting transcript and your reference files. In Settings → AI Providers.
- Provider fallback: when your model fails or stalls, Natively retries it, then moves to your next provider, and tells you which one answered and why the first one failed.
- Background Model: pick a second, faster model for Auto Answer, quick decisions and Fast Response Mode, separate from the Active Model that writes your answers. In Settings → AI Providers.
- New AI providers: Google Antigravity, Fluxion, OpenRouter, 9Router and AgentRouter join the list, and Codex gains GPT-6 Astra and GPT-5.6 Sol.
- Apple Speech (macOS 26 and later): speech-to-text that runs on your Mac, with no API key. Pick it in Settings → Audio and download the languages you need.
- Parakeet TDT local speech: a speech model that runs on your device and covers 25 languages. In Settings → Audio.
- Welcome and shortcut tour: a fresh install opens on a welcome screen, then a three-step tour where you try the main shortcuts for real.
- Usage statistics (on by default): reports which features are used and how often, linked to your install and to your trial or account if you have one. Never what you say, see or type. Turn it off under Advanced in Settings → General.
- Auto Answer now also works when your only provider is a gateway such as OpenRouter or LiteLLM.
- Visual Answers is available in all nine modes, numbers can come back as a chart, and requests are understood in English, Spanish, Russian, Chinese and Japanese.

## macOS Installation (Signed Build)

Download the correct architecture `.dmg` or `.zip` file for your device (Apple Silicon or Intel).

- **For Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4):** Download the `arm64` build.
- **For Intel Macs:** Download the `x64` build.

Open the downloaded file, drag **Natively** to your **Applications** folder, and launch it.

## ⚠️Windows Installation (Unsigned Build)

When running the installer on Windows, you might see a "Windows protected your PC" warning from Microsoft Defender SmartScreen saying it prevented an unrecognized app from starting.

Since this is an unsigned build, this is expected. You can safely ignore it by clicking **More info** and then **Run anyway**.
