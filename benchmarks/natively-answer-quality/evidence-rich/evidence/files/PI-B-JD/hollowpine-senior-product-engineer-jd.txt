Senior Product Engineer — Collaborative AI Workspace
Hollowpine Labs
Rotterdam, Netherlands / Remote (Europe) | Full-time | Posted 22 September 2026 | Ref HPL-2026-031


ABOUT HOLLOWPINE LABS

Hollowpine Labs makes Inkwharf, a collaborative workspace where teams write, plan and sketch together, with an AI assistant built into the document instead of bolted on beside it. We are 85 people, Series B funded, and Inkwharf is used by about 8,500 teams.


THE TEAM

You will join Workspace Surface, the squad that owns the editor, the multiplayer layer and the assistant's in-document interface. The squad is 7 engineers, 2 designers and 1 product manager. You will report to Jorrit Aldewyck, Head of Product Engineering.

Product engineers here are close to the customer: you sit in on research calls, write the experiment plan, ship the feature and read the result.


WHAT YOU WILL WORK ON

- Real-time UI. Presence, live cursors, comments and conflict-free editing on our CRDT layer (Yjs over WebSockets), in documents with up to 50 people editing at once.
- AI UX. Streamed assistant responses inside the document, inline suggestions the user can accept, reject or undo, citations back to the source blocks, and honest states for slow, partial or failed generations.
- Accessibility. The whole workspace to WCAG 2.2 AA, including the hard part: making a multiplayer editor usable with a screen reader.
- Experimentation. We ship behind flags and run around 10 experiments a quarter. Our activation metric is a first shared document within 7 days of sign-up.
- Performance. Interaction delay under 200 ms at p75 on a 500-block document, and an initial JavaScript payload under 350 kB.


WHAT WE NEED

- 6+ years of professional frontend or product engineering, including at least 4 years of React and TypeScript in production.
- You have shipped real-time collaborative or multiplayer features (WebSockets, CRDTs or operational transforms).
- Accessibility work that goes beyond linting: you have taken a complex interface through an audit and fixed what it found.
- You have designed, run and read product experiments, and can say when a result should not be trusted.
- A record of measured frontend performance improvements on real devices.
- Product judgement: you can turn a vague customer problem into a small first version.


NICE TO HAVE

- You have shipped LLM-powered features: streaming interfaces, prompt iteration, or evaluation work with a model team.
- Experience with a rich-text editor framework (ProseMirror, Tiptap or Lexical).
- You have built or maintained a design system.
- Some Node.js. Our backend-for-frontend is a Node.js service that product engineers change regularly.
- Canvas or WebGL rendering.


HOW WE WORK

The role is remote within Europe, in time zones UTC-1 to UTC+3, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 CET. The Rotterdam hub is open to anyone who wants a desk but nobody is required to use it. The whole team meets in Rotterdam for 4 team weeks a year, with travel and hotel paid.

There is no pager rotation. Each engineer is support captain for one week every 7 weeks, during working hours only.

You need the right to work in the European Union. We are not able to sponsor visas for this role.


HIRING PROCESS

1. Intro call, 25 minutes.
2. Pairing session, 90 minutes, on a small React exercise in your own editor.
3. Product and design critique, 45 minutes, with a designer and the product manager.
4. Conversations with two future teammates.

Most candidates finish the process in two weeks.
