# Fennimore Row Homeowners Association

c/o Calloway-Finch Property Management, 200 Harrowgate Road, Suite 4, Halden Cross

## Notice to Owners: Interior Renovation Rules and 2027 Assessments

- Date: 1 September 2026
- From: The Board of Directors
- To: All owners, Units 1 to 36

The Board adopted the rules below at its meeting of 25 August 2026. They replace the renovation guidance circulated in March 2024 and apply to any work that begins on or after 1 October 2026. Please pass a copy to your contractor before work starts.

### 1. When approval is needed

Work that changes a structural or party wall, a shared plumbing stack, the roofline, a window or exterior door opening, or anything visible from outside the unit needs written approval from the Architectural Committee before it starts. Applications go to the management office, and the Committee answers within 30 days.

Interior work that touches none of those does not need Committee approval. This covers replacing a kitchen or bathroom where the fixtures stay on their existing supply and drain locations, new flooring, cabinetry and interior painting. It does need a Renovation Notification Form, filed with the management office at least 14 days before work begins.

If you are not sure which group your project falls into, ask the management office before you sign with a contractor.

### 2. What to file with the form

- The contractor's name, license number, and the expected start and finish dates.
- A certificate of liability insurance for the contractor showing at least $1,000,000 in general liability cover and naming the Association as certificate holder.
- A phone number for someone who can be reached during working hours.

### 3. Working hours

- Monday to Friday: 8:30 am to 5:00 pm.
- Saturday: 10:00 am to 2:00 pm, quiet work only. No demolition and no power saws on Saturdays.
- No contractor work on Sundays or on federal holidays.

Owners are fined $75 for each day on which work takes place outside these hours.

### 4. Dumpsters, deliveries and parking

- A dumpster or storage container may stand only on the owner's own driveway, for no more than 7 consecutive days, and must display a placard from the management office. A $200 refundable damage deposit is taken when the placard is issued.
- Containers may not be left on the street or in the guest lot. A container without a placard is fined $50 per day.
- Contractors may use no more than two spaces in the guest lot. Additional vehicles park on Harrowgate Road.
- Deliveries by trucks longer than 26 feet must be booked with the management office 48 hours ahead so the entrance can be kept clear.

### 5. Common areas

Contractors must sweep the walkway and the shared drive at the end of each working day. Materials may not be stored on the common lawn. Damage to common areas is repaired by the Association and charged to the owner of the unit concerned.

### 6. Assessments for 2027

- Monthly dues rise from $285 to $310 with effect from 1 January 2027.
- A special assessment of $600 per unit for the roof reserve is payable in two installments of $300, due 1 February 2027 and 1 August 2027.
- Owners on autopay need do nothing for the monthly dues. The special assessment is billed separately and is not collected by autopay.

### 7. Annual meeting

The annual meeting of owners is on Thursday 12 November 2026 at 7:00 pm in the clubhouse. Proxy forms must reach the management office by 5 November.
