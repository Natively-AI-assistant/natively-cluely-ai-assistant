## Scope of work and prices

1. Demolition and site protection. Remove the existing cabinets, countertops, backsplash tile and peninsula; protect the floor; haul away debris. $1,700.

2. Cabinetry. Semi-custom maple shaker cabinets, factory painted: 10 base cabinets, 8 wall cabinets and 1 tall pantry cabinet, with soft-close hinges, standard drawer slides, crown molding, toe kicks, delivery and installation. The price includes decorative hardware: up to 38 knobs and pulls from our standard brushed range, fitted. $15,250.

3. Countertops. 2 cm quartz with a built-up edge, approximately 46 sq ft on the perimeter run, one undermount sink cutout, templating and installation. $4,900.

4. Island. Freestanding island measuring 7 ft x 3 ft: three base cabinets, matching quartz top and one dedicated electrical outlet. $5,600.

5. Backsplash. Ceramic subway tile, approximately 28 sq ft, straight lay, grouted and sealed. $1,850.

6. Plumbing and electrical. Reconnect the sink, disposal and dishwasher in their existing locations; two new countertop outlets; four recessed LED lights. $2,950.

7. Appliance allowance. $5,000 toward a range, range hood and dishwasher bought through our supplier. The existing refrigerator is reused.

8. Permit and inspection fees. $600.

Contract total: $37,850. Sales tax on materials is included in the prices above.

## Not included

- Painting of walls and ceiling after installation.
- A new sink or faucet. The owners supply both.
