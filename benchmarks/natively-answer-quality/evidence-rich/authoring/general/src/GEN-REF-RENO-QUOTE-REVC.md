## Scope of work and prices

1. Demolition and site protection. Remove the existing cabinets, countertops, backsplash tile and peninsula; protect the floor and doorways; haul away and dispose of debris. $1,900.

2. Cabinetry. Semi-custom maple shaker cabinets, factory painted in the color chosen at the showroom: 10 base cabinets, 8 wall cabinets and 1 tall pantry cabinet, with soft-close hinges, full-extension soft-close drawer slides, crown molding, toe kicks, one pull-out waste unit, delivery and installation. $14,600. Decorative hardware is not part of this price: the owners supply their own knobs and pulls, and we fit them at no extra charge provided they are on site by the day cabinet installation begins.

3. Countertops. 3 cm quartz, approximately 46 sq ft on the perimeter run, eased edge, one undermount sink cutout, templating and installation. The slab is chosen by the owners before templating. $5,300.

4. Island. Freestanding island measuring 6 ft x 3 ft: two base cabinets, matching quartz top, one dedicated electrical outlet, and the flooring patch under the old peninsula footprint. $4,750. This line is optional and may be removed at any time before acceptance; the contract total and every percentage payment then adjust to match.

5. Backsplash. Ceramic subway tile, approximately 32 sq ft, straight lay, grouted and sealed. $2,150.

6. Plumbing and electrical. Reconnect the sink, disposal and dishwasher in their existing locations; four new countertop outlets with GFCI protection; six recessed LED lights on a dimmer; fitting of the appliances bought under item 7. $3,400.

7. Appliance allowance. $6,000 toward a range, range hood, dishwasher and refrigerator bought through our supplier. The allowance covers purchase only. Anything spent above it is billed at our cost with no markup, and anything left unspent comes off the final invoice as a credit.

8. Permit and inspection fees. Building and electrical permits, filed by us. $650.

Contract total: $38,750. Sales tax on materials is included in the prices above.

## Unforeseen conditions

This quote carries no contingency sum. If we open a wall or floor and find something that could not be seen at the site visit, such as water damage, wiring that does not meet code, or a pipe that is not on the drawings, we stop, show you, and issue a written change order. Change-order work is priced at our cost plus 15% and does not begin until you have approved it in writing. We suggest owners keep roughly 8% of the contract total in reserve for this, about $3,100 on the total above.
