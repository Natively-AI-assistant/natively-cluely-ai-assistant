# Total Rewards Overview

Kestrelwick Systems, Inc. | US full-time employees | Version 4.1 | Effective 1 March 2026

Owner: People Operations (Marguerite Tso-Lindqvist, Head of People Operations)

This overview summarises pay, time away and benefits for full-time employees in the United States. It is written for employees and for candidates who are considering an offer. Where this summary and a plan document differ, the plan document governs.

## Time away

**Paid time off.** Employees receive 24 days of PTO per year. PTO accrues at 2 days per calendar month, starting with the month you join, and can be used as it accrues. Up to 5 unused days carry into the next year.

**Sick time.** 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO. No doctor's note is needed for absences of three days or fewer.

**Company holidays.** Kestrelwick observes 11 company holidays. The list is published each November for the year ahead.

**Winter closure.** The company closes from December 24 through January 1. Closure days are not deducted from PTO.

**Parental leave.** Every new parent receives 18 weeks of fully paid leave, whether the child arrives through birth, adoption or foster placement. There is no distinction between primary and secondary caregivers. Employees are eligible after 90 days of employment. Leave may be taken in one or two blocks within the first year after the child arrives.

**Bereavement and jury duty.** Paid leave is available for both; speak to your People Operations partner.

## Where we work

Kestrelwick is a hybrid company. Employees whose home office is Duluth or Chattanooga are in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday; Monday and Friday are flexible. Fully remote arrangements are limited to roles marked remote-eligible on the requisition. People-manager roles are not remote-eligible.

New hires receive a one-time home-office setup allowance of $600. Parking at the Duluth office is paid by the company.

## Pay

**Base pay.** Base pay is reviewed every April. A base salary range is posted on every requisition.

**Annual bonus.** For lead and staff levels the bonus target is 12% of base salary. For directors and above the bonus target is 18% of base salary. Bonuses are paid in March for the previous calendar year and are prorated for a partial first year.

**Equity.** New hires receive a stock option grant. Options vest over four years: 25% after the first year, then monthly. The size of the grant is set at offer and depends on level.

## Health

Kestrelwick offers three medical plans. The company pays 90% of the employee premium and 70% of dependent premiums. Dental and vision are fully paid for employees. Coverage starts on your first day. Employees on the high-deductible plan receive a company contribution of $1,000 a year to a health savings account.

## Retirement

Kestrelwick matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar, up to 5% of salary. The match vests immediately. You can enrol from your first pay period.

## Growth and wellbeing

- Learning stipend: $2,000 per year for courses, books and conferences.
- Wellness allowance: $50 per month toward a gym, a class or equipment.
- Employee assistance programme: confidential counselling for employees and household members, free of charge.
