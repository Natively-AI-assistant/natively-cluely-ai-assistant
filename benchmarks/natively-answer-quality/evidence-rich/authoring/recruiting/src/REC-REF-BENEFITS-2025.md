# Total Rewards Overview

Kestrelwick Systems, Inc. | US full-time employees | Version 3.2 | Effective 1 February 2025

Prepared by Caspian Ruddock, Total Rewards, for People Operations

This overview describes pay, time away and benefits for full-time employees in the United States, and gives candidates a picture of what joining Kestrelwick looks like. The plan documents govern wherever they differ from this summary.

## Time away

**Paid time off.** Employees receive 20 days of PTO per year, accrued each pay period. Sick time is taken from the same balance. Up to 10 unused days carry into the next year.

**Company holidays.** Kestrelwick observes 10 company holidays.

**Parental leave.** Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Employees are eligible after six months of service.

## Where we work

Kestrelwick is remote-first. Employees may live and work in any of the 23 US states where the company is registered as an employer. The Duluth and Chattanooga offices are optional drop-in spaces, and nobody is expected to attend on set days. Remote employees receive a stipend of $75 per month toward internet and workspace costs.

## Pay

**Base pay.** Base pay is reviewed every January.

**Annual bonus.** For lead and staff levels the bonus target is 10% of base salary. For directors and above the bonus target is 15% of base salary.

**Equity.** Details of any equity grant are set out in your offer letter.

## Health

Kestrelwick offers two medical plans. The company pays 85% of the employee premium and 60% of dependent premiums. Dental and vision are available at employee cost. Coverage starts on the first of the month after your start date.

## Retirement

Kestrelwick matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar, up to 4% of salary. The match vests over two years.

## Growth

- Learning stipend: $1,500 per year.
- Employee assistance programme: confidential counselling, free of charge.

## How hiring works at Kestrelwick

Candidates often ask what to expect, so here is the usual path for engineering roles. There are five stages:

1. A call with a recruiter.
2. A take-home exercise, which most people finish in about 3 hours.
3. An interview with the hiring manager.
4. A virtual panel of three 60-minute sessions.
5. A values conversation with one of the founders.

Most processes take about five weeks from the first call.
