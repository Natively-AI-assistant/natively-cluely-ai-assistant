## About Kestrelwick

Kestrelwick builds Coldline, the routing and temperature-compliance platform that refrigerated freight carriers use to keep food and medicine inside a safe temperature range from dock to door. Every trailer on the platform reports its position, door state and compartment temperature many times a minute. When that stream is late or wrong, a dispatcher somewhere is making a decision blind, so the people who own the stream matter a great deal to us.

## The role

The Ingestion Platform team owns the services that receive, validate and store trailer telemetry before any other Kestrelwick product can use it. As Engineering Lead you are both the team's line manager and its senior technical voice. We expect roughly 60% of your week to go to people and delivery leadership and 40% to hands-on work: design reviews, code review and incident response.

## What you will do

- Line-manage the engineers on the Ingestion Platform team: weekly 1:1s, growth plans, performance reviews and hiring.
- Own the team's ingestion service-level objective: 99.9% of device messages processed within 60 seconds.
- Set technical direction for the streaming backbone and review every design that changes how data enters the platform.
- Join the team's on-call rotation as a regular participant; the lead carries the pager one week in five.
- Act as incident commander for ingestion incidents and run the review that follows.
- Plan each quarter together with the leads of the neighbouring Data Platform teams.
- Travel to the Chattanooga hub for planning weeks, up to four trips per year.

## What you bring (required)

- 7 or more years building backend or data-intensive systems that ran in production.
- At least 2 years of direct people management, including hiring and performance reviews. Mentoring, or tech-lead work without line responsibility, does not count toward this.
- Hands-on production experience operating a log-based streaming platform such as Kafka, Pulsar or Redpanda.
- Experience leading the response to an incident on a customer-facing service as incident commander.
- Fluency in a compiled backend language. We work mostly in Go.

## Nice to have

- Telematics or IoT device data.
- Change data capture pipelines.
- Managing the cost of cloud data infrastructure.
- Multi-region, active-active deployments.

None of the nice-to-have items is needed to apply. If you meet the required list and are unsure about the rest, we would still like to hear from you.
