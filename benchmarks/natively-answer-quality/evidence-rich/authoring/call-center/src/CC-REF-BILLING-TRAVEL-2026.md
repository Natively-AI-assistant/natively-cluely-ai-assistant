# Brindlewatch Billing Guide and Roam Link Rates

Version 2.4 | Effective 1 February 2026 | Owner: Billing Operations
All prices in US dollars, before sales tax

## 1. Keep plans

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Cameras | Clip history |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keep Basic | $6 per month | $60 per year | 1 | 14 days |
| Keep Plus | $13 per month | $130 per year | up to 5 | 30 days |
| Keep Pro | $22 per month | $220 per year | up to 20 | 60 days, plus continuous recording |

Without a plan a camera still gives live view and alerts, but nothing is recorded.

## 2. Billing dates

A monthly plan bills on the same day of each month as the day it started; if a month has no such day, it bills on the last day of that month. An annual plan bills once a year on its start date. Every charge produces an invoice, e-mailed to the login address and listed in the app under Settings > Billing.

## 3. Changing plan mid-month

Moving up on a monthly plan takes effect at once. We charge the difference for the days left: (new monthly price - old monthly price) x days remaining / 30. Every month is treated as 30 days for this purpose.

Example: Keep Basic to Keep Pro with 15 days left is (22 - 6) x 15 / 30 = $8.00 charged today, then $22 from the next billing date.

Moving down on a monthly plan takes effect on the next billing date, with no credit for the current month.

## 4. Roam Link

Roam Link is the cellular service for Trek Cam. It costs $5 per camera per month and covers the United States. Each camera includes 3 GB of data per month, and allowances are not shared between cameras. Extra data is $4 per GB, and a part GB is rounded up to a whole one.

Example: a Trek Cam uses 4.2 GB in a month. It is 1.2 GB over, billed as 2 GB: $8 on top of the monthly fee.

## 5. Using a Trek Cam abroad

Outside the United States a Trek Cam on Roam Link connects through partner networks, and each day abroad carries a Roam Day fee.

| Zone | Countries | Roam Day fee | Cap per camera per billing cycle | Roam Week pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 1 | Canada, Mexico | $10 per camera per day | $60 | $45 per camera |
| Zone 2 | United Kingdom, Ireland, European Union | $15 per camera per day | $90 | $70 per camera |

Roam Link does not work in other countries.

How days are counted:

- A day is a calendar day, midnight to midnight in the account's home time zone - not a 24-hour period from the first connection.
- Any connection to a partner network on a day, however short, makes that day a Roam Day.
- Each Trek Cam is charged separately.
- A camera that is switched off, or set to Wi-Fi only, does not connect and is not charged.
- Data used abroad comes out of the same monthly allowance.

Cap. Roam Day fees stop adding up once they reach the cap for that camera in a billing cycle. The cap counts Roam Day fees only.

Roam Week pass. A pass covers one camera for 7 consecutive calendar days from activation. It has to be activated in the app before the camera first connects abroad and cannot be added to a trip afterwards. Days abroad outside those 7 days are charged as Roam Days.

Examples:

- One camera crosses into Canada at 11:10 pm on the 3rd and comes back at 7:30 am on the 5th. The trip lasted about 32 hours but touched three calendar days: 3 x $10 = $30.
- Three cameras spend 3 calendar days in Ireland: 3 x 3 x $15 = $135.
- One camera spends 9 calendar days in Mexico: 9 x $10 would be more than the Zone 1 cap, so the cap amount is billed.

On the invoice these lines read ROAM DAY Z1 or ROAM DAY Z2, and the quantity is camera-days.

## 6. When a customer disputes travel charges

A Roam Day charge is correct when the connection log shows the camera on a partner network that day. Tier 1 agents see the camera-day count on the invoice but not the log.

First-trip courtesy. If the invoice is the first on the account ever to carry Roam Day fees, a Tier 1 agent may credit up to $30 of them, once per account. Any larger adjustment, or a second request, goes to Billing Operations (Tier 2), who review the connection log. Do not tell the customer what Billing Operations will decide.

## 7. Failed payments

If a charge fails we retry it 3, 6 and 10 days after the failed charge. If the day-10 retry also fails, the plan is suspended. Live view keeps working; clips already recorded are kept for 30 days after suspension and then deleted. Paying the balance within that time restores the plan and the clips.

## 8. Tax and currency

Sales tax is added at the rate for the billing postal code. Customers in Canada are billed in US dollars; their card issuer sets the exchange rate and any foreign-transaction fee.
