# Brindlewatch Refund and Cancellation Policy

Version 3.1 | Effective 1 July 2026
Owner: Marisol Etxeberria, Billing Operations Lead | Approved by: Odalys Ferreira-Novak, Director of Customer Care

## 1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to cameras and Keep subscriptions bought directly from Brindlewatch, on our web store or billed by Brindlewatch in the app. Two kinds of purchase are outside it:

- Subscriptions billed through a phone's app store. We cannot see or reverse those charges; the customer has to ask the app store for the refund.
- Cameras bought from a retail partner. They go back to the retailer under the retailer's own return rules.

## 2. Subscription refunds

| Billing | Rule |
|---|---|
| Annual plan, first purchase or renewal | Full refund if the customer asks within 30 days of the charge date. After that window there is no refund for the unused part of the term; the plan stays active until the term ends and auto-renew can be switched off at any time. |
| Monthly plan | No refund for a partial month. Cancelling stops the next charge and the plan runs to the end of the paid month. One exception: a monthly renewal charge is refunded in full if the customer cancels within 48 hours of that charge. |

Renewal reminders. Annual plans renew automatically. We e-mail a reminder 21 days before the renewal date and again 3 days before. Not seeing a reminder does not extend the refund window.

Moving to a cheaper plan during an annual term. The customer receives a credit for each full unused month: (old annual price - new annual price) / 12 x full months remaining. It is added as account credit against future charges and is not paid back to the card. Moving to a dearer plan is covered in the Billing Guide.

Gift subscriptions cannot be refunded once the recipient has activated them.

## 3. Camera returns

A camera bought directly from us can be returned for any reason within 60 days of the delivery date. We refund the price of the item. The original shipping charge is refunded only when the camera is defective. A return label is free for a defective camera; otherwise $8 is deducted from the refund for the label.

If a subscription was bought in the same order as a camera that is returned in time, the subscription is refunded in full as well.

After the return period there is no refund. Cameras carry a 2-year limited warranty against defects, under which we repair or replace.

## 4. Where the money goes

A refund goes back to the original payment method and nowhere else - not to a different card, a bank account, a gift card or another person. If the original card has since been closed or replaced, the card issuer normally forwards the refund to the customer's new card or account. If the issuer returns the money to us, Billing Operations (Tier 2) arranges a cheque or account credit.

## 5. How long it takes

| Payment method | Time after approval |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Digital wallet | up to 3 business days |
| Direct bank debit | up to 10 business days |
| Account credit | same day |

## 6. Exceptions

A refund outside sections 2 and 3 is possible only in the cases below, and each needs a supervisor's approval whatever the amount:

- The customer was charged after a cancellation we had already confirmed, and has the cancellation e-mail or reference.
- The account holder has died: refund of the full unused months of the current term.
- A defective camera left the subscription unusable while the customer waited for our replacement: refund of the months affected.
- A billing error on our side that Billing Operations has documented.

Forgetting to cancel, not using the service, moving home or switching to another brand are not exceptions.

A duplicate charge - same plan, same amount, same day - is a correction rather than a refund. A Tier 1 agent reverses it on the spot, whatever the amount.

## 7. Who may approve

| Role | May approve |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 agent | Refunds that sections 2 and 3 plainly allow, up to $150 per request |
| Supervisor (Team Lead) | Refunds above $150 and up to $500; every section 6 exception |
| Billing Operations manager | Refunds above $500; any refund by cheque |

When a refund needs a supervisor, the Tier 1 agent submits it and tells the customer three things: that it has been submitted, that a supervisor decides, and that the decision arrives by e-mail within 2 business days. The agent does not say or imply that it will be approved.

## 8. Chargebacks

If the customer has already disputed the charge with their bank, do not refund it as well. Add a note and route the ticket to Billing Operations.

## 9. Tax

Sales tax collected on a refunded amount is refunded with it.
