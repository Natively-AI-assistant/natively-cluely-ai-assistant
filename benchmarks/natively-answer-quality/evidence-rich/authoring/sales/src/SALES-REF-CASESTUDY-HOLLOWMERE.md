# Customer story: Hollowmere Produce Logistics

How a refrigerated carrier cut idling and temperature claims across 212 trucks

Published February 2026 by Kestravane Marketing, with the agreement of Hollowmere Produce Logistics. Figures were supplied by Hollowmere.

## The customer

Hollowmere Produce Logistics is a family-owned refrigerated carrier based in Yakima, Washington. It moves fruit and vegetables from packing houses in the Yakima Valley to grocery distribution centres across the Pacific Northwest. The fleet is 212 refrigerated trucks running out of three depots, with about 260 drivers on the roster.

## The problem

Before Kestravane, Hollowmere tracked its trucks with a basic location service and logged trailer temperatures by hand at each stop. Two things were costing money. Drivers left engines running at docks and rest stops, and nobody could say how much. And when a retailer rejected a load for a temperature excursion, Hollowmere had no continuous record to show where in the journey the load had warmed up, so it usually accepted the claim.

"We were arguing about claims with a clipboard," says Wendeline Aukes, Director of Fleet Operations at Hollowmere. "We lost most of those arguments."

## What Hollowmere bought

Hollowmere runs the Operations plan with two modules: Fuel & Idle Analytics and Cold Chain Monitoring. Each truck carries one TrakNode unit and two TempSense sensors, one at the front of the box and one at the rear doors, which makes 424 sensors in all. Hollowmere did not take cameras or Driver Safety Scoring, and it does not use any of the native connectors; dispatch data moves in and out through scheduled CSV files.

Hollowmere's own maintenance staff fitted the hardware, depot by depot.

## What was measured

Hollowmere compared the six months from 1 March to 31 August 2025, the first full period with every truck reporting, against the same six months of 2024.

| Measure | March to August 2024 | March to August 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average engine idling per truck per day | 73 minutes | 59 minutes | 19% lower |
| Fuel consumed per 100 miles | 16.4 gallons | 15.4 gallons | 6.1% lower |
| Temperature-related load claims | 41 claims | 17 claims | 24 fewer |
| Claims accepted without dispute | 33 claims | 6 claims | 27 fewer |

Hollowmere estimates that the lower fuel consumption was worth about $186,000 over the six months, at the diesel prices it actually paid. Its finance team puts the payback on hardware and first-year subscription at under 9 months.

## How the savings came about

The idle report ranks depots and drivers by minutes of idling per day. Depot managers reviewed it every Monday and spoke to the ten drivers at the top of the list. Aukes says most of the change came in the first eight weeks, once drivers knew the number was being looked at.

On the cold chain side, the temperature excursion alert tells the dispatcher within minutes when either sensor leaves the set range. In several cases the driver was able to fix a door seal or reset the refrigeration unit before the load was lost. Where a claim was still made, the continuous record showed whether the excursion had happened in transit or after delivery.

## What these figures do and do not show

- The figures come from Hollowmere's own records. They have not been audited by Kestravane or by a third party.
- There was no control group. The comparison is the same fleet against its own previous year.
- Hollowmere launched a driver coaching programme in April 2025, during the measured period. Part of the idling and fuel improvement is likely to be due to that programme, and the two effects cannot be separated.
- The dollar estimate depends on diesel prices in 2025 and on Hollowmere's routes. The percentage change in fuel consumed per 100 miles does not depend on fuel price.
- This is one refrigerated fleet in one region. Results for other fleets, vehicle types and climates will differ, and nothing here is a forecast or a promise of results.

## What comes next

Hollowmere is evaluating Maintenance Planner for its 2026 budget and has asked for trailer door sensors, which Kestravane does not currently make.

"The idle number gets the attention," says Aukes, "but the claims are what changed how our customers treat us."
