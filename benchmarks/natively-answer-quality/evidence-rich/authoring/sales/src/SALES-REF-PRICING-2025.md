# Kestravane Fleet - Price List

Version 3.1 - Effective 1 July 2025

Approved by: Cormac Lindqvist-Oyelaran, Director of Revenue Operations. Document owner: Sales Operations.

All amounts in US dollars. For use by Kestravane account teams and authorised resellers in North America. Prices exclude sales tax.

## 1. Subscription plans

| Plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | Minimum fleet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | $29 per vehicle per month, billed annually | $35 per vehicle per month, billed monthly | Minimum 10 vehicles |
| Operations | $42 per vehicle per month, billed annually | $50 per vehicle per month, billed monthly | Minimum 20 vehicles |
| Enterprise | $66 per vehicle per month, billed annually | Not offered, annual contracts only | Minimum 100 vehicles |

A vehicle is any powered asset carrying an active TrakNode unit. Trailers and other unpowered assets are billed at $11 per asset per month on any plan.

Vehicles added during a term are prorated to the end of the term at the contracted rate. Annual billing is invoiced upfront on Net 30 terms. Monthly billing is charged to a card on the first of each month.

## 2. Optional modules

| Module | Price | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| Driver Safety Scoring | $6 per vehicle per month | TrakNode T3 |
| Fuel & Idle Analytics | $4 per vehicle per month | TrakNode T3 |
| Cold Chain Monitoring | $3 per sensor per month | TempSense S2 sensors |

## 3. Onboarding fee

The onboarding fee is charged once, on the first invoice.

| Plan | Onboarding fee |
|---|---|
| Launch | No fee, self-serve setup |
| Operations | $2,000 one-time |
| Enterprise | $6,000 one-time |

The fee covers account configuration and two remote admin training sessions.

## 4. Hardware

Hardware is purchased outright and is invoiced on shipment.

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TrakNode T3 telematics unit | $99 per unit | One required per vehicle |
| TempSense S2 temperature sensor | $64 per sensor | Wireless, pairs with a TrakNode |
| Professional installation | $45 per vehicle | Covers every device fitted in the same visit |

Self-installation carries no charge. Shipping within the contiguous United States is free on orders of 50 units or more.

## 5. Discounts

Discounts apply to plan subscription fees only.

### 5.1 Volume discount

Applied automatically on annual billing.

| Fleet size under contract | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| 100 to 249 vehicles | 5% volume discount |
| 250 to 499 vehicles | 9% volume discount |
| 500 or more vehicles | 14% volume discount |

### 5.2 Approval authority

The thresholds below refer to the total discount off list, with the volume discount counted in.

| Total discount | Who may approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 15% | Account Executive, no further approval needed |
| Above 15%, up to 22% | Regional Sales Director |
| Above 22%, up to 30% | VP Sales |

### 5.3 Pilots

A 14-day pilot of up to 10 vehicles, with hardware on loan, can be offered at the Account Executive's discretion.
