# Device Service and Fit: monthly report for Customer Care

September 2026 | Issued 5 October 2026
Prepared by Florentyn Akabueze-Marsh (Device Service) and Wilhelmina Castellanos-Reid (Fit Programme)
Circulation: Customer Care Team Leads and Tier 1 agents. Figures are for the calendar month unless a line says otherwise. This report describes what happened; it sets no rule, and the service terms and guides remain the place to look for those.

## 1. Headlines

- 1,312 units were scanned in at Marrow Creek, against 1,187 in August. The rise is almost all Eave Cam.
- Dispatch targets were met on 96.4% of standard exchanges and 91.8% of paid repairs.
- Fit partners completed 2,046 visits. Greyling Ford and Yarrowby opened during the month; Brackwater was paused on 28 September.
- The second inspection, new on 1 September, was requested 9 times.

## 2. Claims by route and camera

Accessory exchanges are raised by Tier 1 and do not pass through the bench, so they are not in this table. There were 409 of them, most often a power adapter.

## 3. Bench outcomes

| Outcome | Units | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Fault confirmed | 1,021 | 77.8% |
| No fault found | 148 | 11.3% |
| Customer damage found on a claim inside the warranty | 96 | 7.3% |
| Could not be repaired; renewed unit supplied | 47 | 3.6% |

No fault found. In most of these the camera joined the bench network at the first attempt, which points to the home network and not to the unit. Eleven cameras came back for a second no-fault-found return and carried the handling charge.

Second inspections. Of the 9 requested, 5 upheld the first report, 2 overturned it and 2 were still pending when the month closed. Both overturned reports involved hairline marks on an Eave Cam housing that the second technician judged to be moulding marks.

Repeat faults. The Head of Device Service reviewed 4 cameras that had already been exchanged twice for the same fault code. All 4 were Trek Cams from one production batch, and Tier 2 has been told which batch.

## 4. Dispatch performance

| Measure | Target | September | August |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard exchange: replacement dispatched after scan-in | 4 business days | average 2.6 | average 3.1 |
| Paid repair: unit dispatched after payment | 9 business days | average 6.2 | average 7.4 |
| Advance exchange: faulty unit scanned in after the replacement left | 20 calendar days | average 11.5 | average 12.2 |

Paid repairs missed the target mostly in the second week, when a delivery of Trek Cam lens assemblies arrived late. Of 181 advance-exchange holds, 7 became a charge because the faulty unit was never sent in.

Repair quotes. 323 quotes were sent. 271 were accepted and paid, 38 lapsed and the units went back unrepaired, and 14 were still open at the end of the month.

## 5. Where agents can help

- 63 parcels arrived with accessories in the box. They cannot be matched back to a customer once the camera has gone to the bench. Please keep telling customers to send the camera only.
- 29 parcels arrived with no Return Authorisation number on the label and went back to the sender unopened.
- 112 claims waited for proof of purchase before they could move. They sat for an average of 3.4 days. Asking for the order number or the receipt on the first contact removes most of that wait.
- Notes marked for the Device Service desk were answered by the end of the next business day in all but 6 cases.

## 6. Contacts about open claims

Tier 1 took 2,874 contacts from customers with a claim open, about 2.2 for every unit scanned in.

| Reason for the contact | Share |
|---|---|
| Where the unit or the replacement has got to | 41% |
| The repair quote: what it covers, how to pay | 22% |
| A card hold that the customer expected to have gone | 9% |
| Disagreement with a bench outcome | 6% |
| Everything else | 22% |

On card holds: the desk releases a hold on the day the faulty unit is scanned in, but the customer's bank decides when the release shows on a statement. Agents can see the release date on the claim record and can give the customer that date.

## 7. Fit

| Partner | Visits completed | Arrived inside the window | Missed visits | Late cancellations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thornapple Home Services | 1,283 | 94.1% | 31 | 58 |
| Kestrel & Vane Installations | 763 | 89.7% | 27 | 44 |
| Both partners | 2,046 | 92.5% | 58 | 102 |

The 2,046 visits fitted 4,410 cameras, about 2.2 a visit; 388 of those cameras were high mounts.

Kestrel & Vane's arrival figure was pulled down by Cinder Bluff and Yarrowby, where one crew covers long distances. The partner is adding a second vehicle in Sablecrest.

Late fee. The Fit Programme desk received 37 waiver requests and granted 12, nearly all where a customer had been taken into hospital or a parcel had been misrouted. A request with the reason written out is decided faster than one that says only that the customer is unhappy.

Workmanship. Partners returned to 19 addresses under the guarantee, most often for a mount on render that had worked loose.

Property. 6 reports of damage were passed to the partners. None has been closed yet.

Returns. 71 cameras fitted under Fit were sent back for a refund during the month, out of roughly 4,400 fitted.
