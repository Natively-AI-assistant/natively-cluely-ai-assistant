Dear Dr Thornquist,

We are pleased to tell you that paper 118, "TERN: Calibrated Fault Detection for School Air-Handling Units" (Thornquist, Raskinen and Brennecke), has been ACCEPTED for the proceedings and selected for ORAL presentation.

HOW THE DECISION WAS REACHED
The symposium received 152 full papers by the deadline. Each was read by three members of the programme committee. Reviews were completed on 3 July, the committee met on 8 July, and all corresponding authors are being notified today. SBSA has no rebuttal stage. Accepted papers that were not selected for oral presentation are presented as posters; their authors are being written to separately.

SCORES FOR PAPER 118
Each reviewer scores four criteria from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

  Reviewer   Relevance   Technical quality   Clarity   Overall
  R1         5           4                   3         4
  R2         4           4                   4         4
  R3         5           3                   4         4

SUMMARY BY THE AREA CHAIR
All three reviewers recommend acceptance. They agree that the paper addresses a problem the symposium's audience meets every working day and that the evaluation is careful. The requests below concern presentation. Only the two marked REQUIRED are conditions of acceptance; the others are left to your judgement.

REVIEWER 1
(a) REQUIRED. The axis labels and the legend of Figure 3 cannot be read at print size. Please redraw the figure.
(b) Several abbreviations are used before they are defined. Please define each at first use.
(c) The symbols for the residuals are not the same in the method section and in the section on isolation. One notation throughout would help.

REVIEWER 2
(a) The introduction is long for a paper of this length. I would shorten it by half a page and give the space to the experiments.
(b) A small table of notation would save the reader some page-turning.
(c) A list of typographical errors is attached to this review in the submission system.

REVIEWER 3
(a) REQUIRED. The header of Table 1 must state the unit of the delay column.
(b) The background section overlooks two earlier SBSA papers on rule-based diagnostics. References are given in the submission system.
(c) The results by fault class are in the supplement only. One sentence in the main text pointing to them would be enough.

FINAL VERSION
- Due Monday 21 September 2026, 23:59 UTC, through the submission system. There are no extensions.
- Length: 8 pages including figures and tables, plus up to one further page for references and acknowledgements.
- Supplementary material: one PDF, not typeset by us, published alongside the paper.
- Use the SBSA 2026 two-column template without changing margins or type size.
- A copyright form signed by one author must be uploaded with the final version.
