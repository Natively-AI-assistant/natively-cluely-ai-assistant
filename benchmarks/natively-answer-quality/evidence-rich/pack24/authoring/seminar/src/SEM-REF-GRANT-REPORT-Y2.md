## 1 The year in brief

Year 2 was the year in which the project moved from collecting to concluding. Trend-log collection closed as planned, the labelled fault set was finished, the detector was run live with the facilities team, and the first full paper was written and accepted. Alongside the fault-detection strand, the classroom air-quality strand that the Trust added at the year-1 review put loggers into classrooms for a whole term.

Two things did not go to plan. The estate's BMS vendor changed its export tool in February, which cost the project most of a month in re-exporting and re-checking files, and the classroom strand's analysis is later than hoped because two loggers failed their end-of-term check and their data had to be cut back by hand.

## 3 Classroom air-quality strand

The strand is led by Juho Raskinen as part of his doctoral work. Loggers were in 36 classrooms of six primary and infant schools from 12 January to 27 March 2026.

Only an interim figure is available for this report. On the readings from the first half of the term (12 January to 13 February), before the end-of-term logger checks and before days with moved or unplugged loggers were removed, the carbon dioxide concentration was above 1,500 ppm for 26% of teaching hours. This is an interim figure and will change: it covers the coldest five weeks only, and it includes readings that the final cleaning will drop. The full-term result will be reported in the thesis chapter, which goes to the supervisory panel in September.

Head teachers have been told that school-level results will be shared with them before anything is published.

## 7 Working with the council

The council's Estates and Facilities team met the project once a month through the year. Three points from those meetings are worth recording for the Trust.

First, the facilities team's time is the scarce resource. Requests that needed someone from the team on site were grouped into as few visits as possible.

Second, the estate's BMS vendor changed its export tool in February 2026. The laboratory's export script was rewritten in the same month and the affected files were re-exported; nothing was lost.

Third, head teachers were more willing to host classroom loggers than expected. Six of the nine schools approached agreed, against a planning assumption of four.

## 8 Ethics, consent and safeguarding

The classroom strand was approved by the Institute's research ethics committee on 4 December 2025 (reference KIT-REC-2025-117). The loggers record carbon dioxide, temperature and humidity only; they record no sound and no images. Parents were told about the study by a letter sent through each school, and each head teacher signed a site agreement before any logger was installed. Everyone who entered a classroom for the project held a current enhanced background check, and all installation and retrieval visits took place outside teaching hours.

No complaint or incident was reported during the term.
