# DRAFT - NOT APPROVED - Kestravane Fleet Price List 2027 (proposal)

Draft 0.3 - prepared 22 September 2026 by the Pricing Committee working group (lead: Fenwick Adeyoola, Pricing Strategy).

Proposed effective date: 1 January 2027, subject to approval by the VP Revenue Operations and the CFO. Nothing in this document is approved. Do not quote from it. Until a new list is signed off, version 4.2 remains the only price list in force.

## Why we are proposing changes

Hosting and cellular data costs rose during 2026 while unit costs for hardware came down. The working group proposes to raise the two upper subscription tiers, pass part of the hardware saving on to customers, and tighten field discount authority, which has eroded realised price on mid-size deals. Launch is left alone to protect the entry point.

## Proposed subscription plans

| Plan | Annual billing (proposed) | Monthly billing (proposed) | Minimum fleet (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | $29 per vehicle per month, billed annually, unchanged | $35 per vehicle per month, billed monthly, unchanged | Minimum 10 vehicles, unchanged |
| Operations | $51 per vehicle per month, billed annually | $60 per vehicle per month, billed monthly | Minimum 30 vehicles |
| Enterprise | $78 per vehicle per month, billed annually | Not offered | Minimum 150 vehicles, unchanged |

## Proposed modules

Driver Safety Scoring, Fuel & Idle Analytics, Maintenance Planner and Cold Chain Monitoring stay at their current prices.

| New module | Proposed price | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Route Optimizer | $9 per vehicle per month | Not released. Planned general availability Q1 2027, pending the product readiness review |

Route Optimizer is not on the current price list and must not be offered or priced to customers until the readiness review has passed and this list is approved.

## Proposed hardware and onboarding

| Item | Proposed price |
|---|---|
| TrakNode T4 telematics unit | $79 per unit |
| RoadLens dual-facing camera | $199 per camera |
| TempSense S2 temperature sensor | $64 per sensor, unchanged |
| Operations onboarding fee | $3,000 one-time |
| Enterprise onboarding fee | $8,500 one-time |

## Proposed discount authority

Volume discount bands stay as they are. The approval thresholds would tighten:

| Total discount | Who may approve (proposed) |
|---|---|
| Up to 7% | Account Executive |
| Above 7%, up to 16% | Regional Sales Director |
| Above 16%, up to 24% | VP Sales, after Deal Desk review |

The working group also proposes raising the cap on renewal increases to 7.5% per year.

## Open points for the committee

1. Whether existing Operations customers below 30 vehicles are grandfathered at the 25-vehicle minimum.
2. Whether the hardware reductions should take effect earlier than the subscription changes.
3. Finance has not yet modelled the revenue effect of the tighter Account Executive threshold.
4. Legal review of the renewal-cap wording is outstanding.

Comments to the working group by 16 October 2026. The committee meets on 28 October 2026; nothing here should be treated as decided before then.
