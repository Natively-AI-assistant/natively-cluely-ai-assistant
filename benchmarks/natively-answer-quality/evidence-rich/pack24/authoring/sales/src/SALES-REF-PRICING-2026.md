# Kestravane Fleet - Price List

Version 4.2 - Effective 1 July 2026 - Supersedes all 3.x price lists

Approved by: Odalys Brennecke, VP Revenue Operations. Document owner: Deal Desk (dealdesk@kestravane.example).

All amounts in US dollars. For use by Kestravane account teams and authorised resellers in North America. Prices exclude sales tax.

## 1. Subscription plans

| Plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | Minimum fleet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | $29 per vehicle per month, billed annually | $35 per vehicle per month, billed monthly | Minimum 10 vehicles |
| Operations | $47 per vehicle per month, billed annually | $55 per vehicle per month, billed monthly | Minimum 25 vehicles |
| Enterprise | $72 per vehicle per month, billed annually | Not offered, annual contracts only | Minimum 150 vehicles |

A vehicle is any powered asset carrying an active TrakNode unit. Trailers and other unpowered assets are billed at $11 per asset per month on any plan.

A fleet smaller than the plan minimum may still subscribe to that plan, but it is billed for the minimum fleet size. Vehicles added during a term are prorated to the end of the term at the contracted rate.

Annual billing is invoiced upfront on Net 30 terms. Monthly billing is charged to a card on the first of each month and can be cancelled with one full billing cycle of written notice.

The contracted rate is locked for the length of the term. At renewal, increases on annual contracts are capped at 6% per year.

## 4. Onboarding fee

The onboarding fee is charged once, on the first invoice.

| Plan | Onboarding fee |
|---|---|
| Launch | No fee, self-serve setup |
| Operations | $2,500 one-time |
| Enterprise | $7,500 one-time |

The fee covers the kickoff call, account configuration, two remote admin training sessions and support through go-live. It does not cover hardware installation.

## 5. Hardware

Hardware is purchased outright and is invoiced on shipment.

| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TrakNode T4 telematics unit | $89 per unit | One required per vehicle |
| TempSense S2 temperature sensor | $64 per sensor | Wireless, pairs with a TrakNode |
| RoadLens dual-facing camera | $229 per camera | Road and cab view |
| Professional installation | $45 per vehicle | Covers every device fitted in the same visit |

Self-installation carries no charge. Shipping within the contiguous United States is free on orders of 20 units or more; smaller orders ship for $75 flat. All hardware carries a 3-year warranty with advance replacement.

## 6. Discounts

Discounts apply to plan subscription fees only. Modules, add-ons, hardware, installation and onboarding fees are never discounted.

### 6.1 Volume discount

The volume discount is applied automatically on annual billing. Monthly billing is not eligible.

| Fleet size under contract | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| 100 to 249 vehicles | 5% volume discount |
| 250 to 499 vehicles | 8% volume discount |
| 500 or more vehicles | 12% volume discount |

### 6.2 Approval authority

The thresholds below refer to the total discount off list, with the volume discount counted in.

| Total discount | Who may approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account Executive, no further approval needed |
| Above 10%, up to 18% | Regional Sales Director |
| Above 18%, up to 25% | VP Sales, after Deal Desk review |
| Above 25% | Not available without a written exception from the CFO |

A quote that exceeds the signer's authority is not valid until the approval is recorded in the Deal Desk queue. Committing to a three-year term locks the rate for the whole term but does not add a further percentage discount.

### 6.3 Pilots and special schedules

A 21-day pilot of up to 5 vehicles, with hardware on loan, can be offered with sign-off from the Regional Sales Director. Municipal and non-profit fleets are quoted from the Public Sector schedule, which is maintained separately.

## 7. Plan highlights
