# Kestravane Partner Program Guide

Program year 2026-27 - Version 2.0 - Effective 1 July 2026 - Replaces the 2025-26 program overview

Approved by: Leontyne Abernethy-Osei, Head of Partnerships. Maintained by Channel Operations (partners@kestravane.example). Written for Kestravane partners and for the account teams who work alongside them. All amounts in US dollars.

## 1. What the program is for

Kestravane sells Kestravane Fleet through its own account teams and through partners. This guide sets out who can be a partner, what each kind of partner does, how partners are paid, and how partners and the direct team stay out of each other's way. It applies to companies established in the United States and Canada.

The program year runs from 1 July to 30 June. The rates, thresholds and benefits below apply to the whole program year and are reviewed once a year. This guide explains the program; the partner agreement is the contract.

## 2. Partner types at a glance

| Partner type | What the partner does | Who contracts with the customer | How the partner is paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referral partner | Introduces a prospect, then steps back | Kestravane | A referral fee, paid once |
| Reseller | Sells Kestravane Fleet subscriptions to its own customers and bills them | The reseller | A resale margin on what it resells |
| Installation partner | Fits hardware to vehicles on Kestravane's behalf | Kestravane | A per-vehicle rate set out in the partner's installation schedule |

A company can be more than one kind of partner.

## 3. Referral partners

Any business that meets fleet operators can apply: insurance brokers, fuel suppliers, body builders, maintenance shops, consultants. There is no certification requirement and no revenue target. The partner accepts the referral agreement in the partner portal and can register introductions from that day.

The partner earns a referral fee of 9% of the plan subscription fees billed to the referred customer for its first twelve months, calculated after any discount. Fees for modules, add-ons, hardware, installation and onboarding are left out of the calculation. The fee is limited to $20,000 for any one referred customer.

The referral fee is paid once. Nothing further is paid on renewals, or on vehicles the customer adds after its first twelve months.

An introduction qualifies when:

- the prospect operates at least 12 vehicles;
- Kestravane has had no open opportunity with the prospect in the previous 9 months;
- the partner registered the introduction (section 5) before Kestravane's first meeting with the prospect; and
- the prospect becomes a paying customer while the registration is still protected.

The fee becomes payable once the customer has paid its first bill in full, and it appears on the partner's next quarterly statement. If the customer cancels or stops paying within its first 4 months the fee is forfeited, and a fee already paid is deducted from later statements.

## 4. Resellers

A reseller sells in its own name. It quotes from the Kestravane price list in force, takes the customer's order, bills the customer, and is billed in turn by Kestravane less its resale margin. A reseller has no discount authority of its own: any discount to an end customer follows the approval rules of the price list and is requested through the reseller's Partner Manager.

### 4.1 Tiers

| | Registered | Certified | Premier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription revenue sourced in the previous program year | No minimum | $120,000 | $450,000 |
| Staff with the sales certification | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Staff with the technical certification | None required | 1 | 3 |
| Resale margin on first-year subscriptions | 19% | 23% | 27% |
| Resale margin on renewals | 2% | 3% | 4% |
| Demo kits on loan | None | 2 kits | 6 kits |
| Co-marketing funds | Not available | Up to $6,500 a year | Up to $16,000 a year |
| Partner Manager | Channel desk | Named | Named, with a joint plan each quarter |

The resale margin is earned on plan subscription fees and on module fees, calculated after any discount. Hardware, installation, onboarding fees and add-on services carry no margin; the reseller passes them on at the listed price.

### 4.2 Moving between tiers

Tiers are set at the annual review in July, on the subscription revenue the reseller sourced in the program year just ended. A reseller that passes the next threshold during the year, and has the certified staff, can be promoted without waiting for the review; promotions are approved by the reseller's Partner Manager. A reseller that falls short at the review keeps its tier for the first half of the new program year and drops a tier at the half-year point if it has not caught up.

### 4.3 Certification

The sales certification is an online course of six modules with an examination; the pass mark is 80%. The technical certification adds a hands-on installation day at the Kansas City training bay and a workshop on the REST API. A certification belongs to the person, not to the company, and is valid for 18 months. The sales certification costs $350 per person and the technical certification $900 per person. Both are credited back to a reseller that reaches Certified within the same program year.

## 5. Deal registration

Every partner-sourced opportunity, referral or resale, starts with a registration in the partner portal. The partner gives the prospect's legal name, its depot addresses, an estimate of the fleet, the contact it is working with and the date of its last conversation.

An accepted registration is protected for 120 days from the date of acceptance. During that time no other partner can register the same prospect, and the direct team does not open a competing opportunity. If the prospect contacts Kestravane on its own while a registration is protected, the account executive brings the registering partner into the conversation.

Protection can be extended once, by 30 days, where the prospect has confirmed in writing that its evaluation is still running. Extensions are granted by the partner's Partner Manager. A registration with no logged activity for 60 days is closed, whether or not its protection has run out.

A registration is turned down when:

- Kestravane or another partner already has an open opportunity with the prospect, or had one in the previous 9 months;
- the prospect is on the direct named-account list published in the portal at the start of each quarter; or
- the prospect is already a Kestravane customer, in which case added vehicles are an expansion and not a new opportunity.

Channel Operations reviews registrations in the order in which they arrive.

## 6. Leads passed from Kestravane

Kestravane passes some of its own inbound enquiries to resellers. An enquiry from a fleet of fewer than 35 vehicles, in a state or province where a Certified or Premier reseller is active, is offered to those resellers in rotation. Registered resellers do not receive passed leads.

The reseller that accepts a passed lead makes first contact within four working days and records the outcome in the portal. A lead that is not contacted in that time goes back to the direct team and is not offered again. A passed lead earns the reseller its normal margin; it does not count as sourced revenue for the tier thresholds.

## 7. Working with the direct sales team

Account executives are paid the same commission on a partner-sourced deal as on a direct one, so no account executive gains by working around a partner. A reseller's registered opportunities are visible to the account executive for the territory, who joins customer meetings when the reseller asks and handles anything that needs Kestravane's approval, such as pricing off the list or a change to contract terms.

Partners do not make commitments on Kestravane's behalf. A partner has no authority to vary Kestravane's subscription terms, its service levels or its delivery dates, and nothing a partner tells a customer binds Kestravane unless Kestravane confirms it in writing.

## 8. Customers who buy through a reseller

A customer that buys through a reseller gets the same service as a customer that buys direct.

- Contract. The customer accepts Kestravane's subscription terms as part of the reseller's order form. The reseller bills the customer.
- Onboarding. Onboarding is delivered by Kestravane Professional Services, in the same way as for a direct customer on the same plan. Resellers do not run onboarding and do not quote go-live dates.
- Support. The customer contacts Kestravane support directly, through the channels and during the hours of its plan. The reseller is not a first line of support and is not expected to triage tickets.
- Service levels. The Support Plans and Service Level Guide applies to resold customers exactly as it does to direct customers. Where a service credit is due, Kestravane issues it to the reseller as a credit note, and the reseller must pass the whole amount on to the customer on its next bill.
- Renewals. The reseller manages the renewal conversation and earns the renewal margin for its tier for as long as it remains the customer's reseller.

## 9. Installation partners

Installation partners fit TrakNode units, sensors and cameras for customers who have ordered professional installation. Customers always book and pay for installation through Kestravane; an installation partner does not quote customers and does not bill them.

Every fitter sent to a customer site carries a Kestravane installer certificate, earned on a two-day course and renewed every 18 months. Work follows the current installation guide, and for each vehicle the fitter uploads the photographs that guide asks for before leaving the site.

If a fault traced to the installation appears within 28 days, the installation partner returns to put it right at its own cost. Installation partners carry their own liability insurance, with cover of at least $2 million per occurrence.

## 10. Co-marketing funds and demo kits

Co-marketing funds pay for activity that produces registered opportunities: a stand at a regional trade show, a customer breakfast, local advertising. The reseller sends a short plan to its Partner Manager before spending anything and claims the cost, with receipts, by the end of the quarter after the activity. The amounts in the tier table are annual ceilings, not entitlements, and money not claimed in the program year lapses.

A demo kit contains one TrakNode T4, one RoadLens camera, two TempSense S2 sensors and a bench power supply. Kits remain Kestravane's property and are for demonstrations on the partner's own bench or vehicles. They are not lent to prospects.

## 11. Statements and pipeline reviews

Each partner receives a statement in the portal during the first month of every quarter. It lists the referral fees that became payable, the margin on subscriptions billed, co-marketing claims paid and any deductions. A partner has until the end of that quarter to query a statement.

Premier resellers review their pipeline with their Partner Manager every month, Certified resellers every quarter. Registered resellers are reviewed once a year, at the annual review.

## 12. Enterprise opportunities

The Enterprise plan is always contracted by Kestravane directly. It is not resold, whatever the reseller's tier, because Enterprise customers negotiate contract terms that only Kestravane can agree.

A reseller can still register an opportunity that turns out to need Enterprise. When a reseller's registered opportunity closes on the Enterprise plan, the reseller earns a co-sell fee of 13% of the first-year plan subscription fees, calculated after any discount, in place of any resale margin. The co-sell fee is the same for Registered, Certified and Premier resellers. It is paid once; no margin and no further fee is paid when the customer renews.

The account executive leads an Enterprise opportunity from the point at which the plan is confirmed, and the reseller stays involved under a short joint account plan. Enterprise revenue counts towards the reseller's tier threshold at half its value.

Referral partners are unaffected by this section: a referral that closes on Enterprise earns the referral fee described in section 3.

## 13. Disputes and exceptions

Most overlaps are settled by the registration date. Where they are not, for example where two partners each claim to have introduced the same prospect, or where a partner contests a rejected registration or a named-account ruling, the matter goes to the Director of Channel Operations, Hyacinth Morgenroth. Her decision is final. Partner Managers do not rule on disputes that involve their own partners.

Exceptions to the rates in this guide, such as a margin outside the tier table for a strategic opportunity, need the written agreement of the Head of Partnerships before the reseller quotes.

## 14. Conduct, brand and customer data

- Partners describe themselves as a "Kestravane Partner" with their tier, and never as Kestravane. The partner badge may be used on websites and quotations; the Kestravane logo on its own may not.
- Partners do not bid on Kestravane brand terms in paid search.
- A partner has no access to a customer's Kestravane account unless one of the customer's administrators creates a user for it. Partner staff with such access act on the customer's instructions.
- Gifts and hospitality offered to a prospect are limited to $80 per person per occasion.

## 15. Leaving the program

Either side can end a partner's participation with 50 days' written notice. Margin on subscriptions already billed is unaffected. Customers of a departing reseller move to Kestravane direct, or to another reseller of their choice, at their next renewal. Referral fees that have already become payable are paid on the next quarterly statement.

A Registered reseller with no accepted registration for 9 consecutive months is set to inactive and loses portal access until it asks to be reinstated.
