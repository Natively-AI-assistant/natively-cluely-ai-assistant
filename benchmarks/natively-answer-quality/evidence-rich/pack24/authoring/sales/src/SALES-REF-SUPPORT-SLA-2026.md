# Kestravane Fleet - Support Plans and Service Level Guide

Revision 3 - Effective 1 July 2026

Approved by: Marisol Achterberg, VP Customer Operations. This guide describes the standard support and service level terms attached to each subscription plan. It is referenced by the Master Subscription Agreement.

## 1. Support by plan

| Plan | Channels | Hours | Languages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | Email and help centre | Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 Central | English |
| Operations | Email and chat | 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday | English, Spanish |
| Enterprise | Phone, email and chat | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | English, Spanish, French |

Phone support is offered on Enterprise only. Operations customers who call the main line are asked to open a chat or email ticket.

## 2. Severity levels

- Priority 1: the service is unavailable, or vehicle data has stopped arriving for more than a quarter of the fleet.
- Priority 2: a major function is impaired and no workaround exists.
- Priority 3: a minor fault, a how-to request or a cosmetic problem.

The support engineer assigns the severity. A customer may ask for it to be reviewed by the duty lead.

## 3. First response targets

| Plan | Priority 1 | Priority 2 | Priority 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | Priority 1: 4 business hours | Priority 2: 1 business day | Priority 3: 3 business days |
| Operations | Priority 1: 1 hour | Priority 2: 4 business hours | Priority 3: 1 business day |
| Enterprise | Priority 1: 30 minutes | Priority 2: 2 hours | Priority 3: 8 business hours |

These are targets for a first response from a support engineer, measured within the support hours of the plan. They are not resolution times and they carry no service credit.

## 4. Uptime

Uptime is measured per calendar month for the web application and the API, excluding scheduled maintenance.

| Plan | Monthly uptime commitment | Service credit |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | No commitment | None |
| Operations | 99.5% | 5% of the monthly subscription fee if uptime is below 99.5%; 10% if it is below 99.0% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% | 10% of the monthly subscription fee if uptime is below 99.95%; 25% if it is below 99.0% |

For annual contracts the monthly subscription fee is one-twelfth of the annual plan fee. A credit must be claimed in writing within 45 days of the end of the affected month and is applied to the next invoice; credits are not paid out in cash. Service credits are the sole remedy for missed uptime.

Scheduled maintenance takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 05:00 Central, is announced 5 business days ahead, and is limited to 6 hours in any month. Beta features, the customer's own network and third-party systems on the other end of a connector are outside the uptime commitment.

## 5. Changes to standard terms

The uptime figures above are the only ones Kestravane offers. A different uptime figure, a different credit schedule or a response-time commitment in a contract is a non-standard term. Non-standard terms need written approval from the VP Customer Operations and from Legal, requested through Deal Desk, before they are put in front of a customer. Account teams must not offer or imply service levels other than those in this guide.

## 6. Customer success coverage

| Plan | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Launch | Help centre and email |
| Operations | Pooled Customer Success team, with a group check-in webinar each quarter |
| Enterprise, 300 or more vehicles | Named Customer Success Manager, with a business review each quarter |
| Enterprise, fewer than 300 vehicles | Pooled Customer Success team, with a business review twice a year |

A Technical Account Manager can be added to any Enterprise account as a paid add-on; see the price list.

## 7. Escalation

1. A Priority 1 ticket that is not resolved within 2 hours is escalated to the Duty Incident Manager.
2. At 4 hours the VP Customer Operations is notified and joins the incident channel.
3. Enterprise customers receive an update every hour during a Priority 1 incident; Operations customers every 4 hours.
4. Any customer can ask for an escalation at any time by replying "escalate" on the ticket or, on Enterprise, by calling the escalation line printed on the welcome letter.

Current incidents and maintenance notices are posted on status.kestravane.example.

## 8. Hardware returns

A TrakNode, sensor or camera that fails under warranty is replaced in advance: the replacement ships within 2 business days of the fault being confirmed, with a prepaid label for the faulty unit. Units damaged by collision, water ingress beyond the rated level or unauthorised opening are not covered.

## 9. What support does not cover

- Building or debugging the customer's own code against the API.
- Editing connector field mappings on the customer's behalf.
- On-site visits, which are quoted separately as training or professional services.
