# ECN 1620 Prices, Markets and Money: formula sheet

## A. Elasticity

- Percentage change = (new - old) / old, x 100. Use the starting value as the base.
- Price elasticity of demand (PED) = % change in quantity demanded / % change in price. Keep the minus sign.
- Elastic: PED below -1. Unit elastic: PED equal to -1. Inelastic: PED between -1 and 0.
- Cross-price elasticity = % change in quantity of good A / % change in price of good B. Positive: substitutes. Negative: complements.
- Income elasticity = % change in quantity / % change in income. Negative: inferior good. Between 0 and 1: necessity. Above 1: luxury.
- Price elasticity of supply = % change in quantity supplied / % change in price.
- Total revenue = price x quantity.

## B. Prices

- CPI in year t = cost of the basket in year t / cost of the basket in the base year, x 100.
- Inflation rate = (CPI this year - CPI last year) / CPI last year, x 100.
- Real rate = nominal rate - inflation, when inflation is below 10%. At 10% or more: (1 + nominal) = (1 + real) x (1 + inflation).
- Real value = nominal value / CPI, x 100.

## C. Money and policy

- Quantity equation: M x V = P x Y. Growth form: money growth + velocity growth = inflation + real output growth.
- Money multiplier = 1 / reserve ratio.
- Taylor rule: policy rate = neutral real rate + inflation + 0.5 x (inflation - target) + 0.5 x output gap. Use a neutral real rate of 2% and a target of 2% unless told otherwise.

## D. Rounding

- All results: 1 decimal place.
- Round only at the end.

## E. Symbols

- P price, Q quantity, TR total revenue.
- M money supply, V velocity of circulation, Y real output.
- CPI consumer price index, with the base year set to 100.
- The output gap is actual output less potential output, as a percentage of potential. A negative gap means the economy is producing below potential.

## F. Reminders

- State the method in one line before you calculate. A bare number earns no method marks.
- Give units. An interest rate is in percent a year; an elasticity has no units.
