## 3. What the course covers

The course follows a packet from the wire to the application and back. In order, the first half of the term covers: the layered model; links, frames and error detection; switching and Ethernet; sockets and the transport layer; DNS; HTTP; TCP congestion control; TCP reliability and retransmission; IP addressing and subnets; routing inside and between networks; and TLS. The second half covers wireless networks, network security and data-centre networking. A dated lecture plan is posted on the course site in the first week.

If you have a learning support plan, send it to the lecturer in the first two weeks of term so that exam arrangements can be made in time.
