# Brindlewatch Repair and Replacement Service Terms

Version 2.2 | Effective 1 September 2026
Owner: Florentyn Akabueze-Marsh, Head of Device Service | Approved by: Odalys Ferreira-Novak, Director of Customer Care
Applies to: Lantern Cam, Eave Cam and Trek Cam, and to accessories sold under the Brindlewatch name

## 1. What these terms are for

These terms describe what happens once a camera has developed a fault in use: how a claim is opened, how the unit travels, what the customer receives and what, if anything, it costs. They are the wording Customer Care relies on when a customer asks.

They do not set the length of the limited warranty, which is stated in the Refund and Cancellation Policy and printed on the product box. They also leave two neighbouring cases to other documents:

- a camera the customer simply wants to send back for their money - Refund and Cancellation Policy;
- a parcel that arrives damaged, or does not arrive - Shipping and Delivery Policy.

## 2. Who decides

A claim under these terms starts only when Tier 2 Device Diagnostics has confirmed a hardware fault and recorded a fault code. Tier 1 agents do not diagnose hardware and do not open a claim on their own judgement, however obvious the fault sounds. Once the fault code is recorded, Device Diagnostics issues the Return Authorisation and chooses the service route in section 3. Tier 1 can read the route and its progress in the support console and explain both to the customer.

Nothing in these terms changes when or how a suspected fault is passed to Device Diagnostics. That is set by the Escalation Matrix and the Outage and Troubleshooting Guide.

## 3. Service routes

| Route | When it is used | What the customer receives |
|---|---|---|
| Standard exchange | A fault inside the warranty; the default route | A replacement unit, dispatched no later than 4 business days after the faulty unit is scanned in at the service centre |
| Advance exchange | A fault inside the warranty, where the customer asks not to be left without a camera and agrees to a card hold | A replacement unit dispatched first; the faulty unit follows and must be scanned in within 20 calendar days |
| Paid repair | A fault outside the warranty, or damage that is not a defect (section 6) | The customer's own unit, repaired for the flat fee in section 7 |

A replacement unit is either new or renewed. A renewed unit is one that came back to us, was rebuilt with a new housing seal and lens cover, and passed the same bench checks as a new unit. The customer cannot choose between the two, and agents do not say which will be sent.

Every replacement unit is covered for the remainder of the original warranty or for 90 days from its delivery, whichever is longer. An exchange does not start a new warranty: the date on the proof of purchase goes on defining it.

## 4. Advance exchange

Only Device Diagnostics authorises an advance exchange. Tier 1 may tell a customer that the route exists, but not that it will be granted. It is offered once the fault code is recorded and the customer agrees to a temporary hold on a payment card.

| Camera | Hold placed on the card |
|---|---|
| Lantern Cam | $65 |
| Eave Cam | $115 |
| Trek Cam | $185 |

The hold is an authorisation, not a charge, and it is released when the faulty unit is scanned in at the service centre. The 20 calendar days run from the day the replacement is dispatched. If the faulty unit has not been scanned in by then, the hold becomes a charge and the claim is closed. A customer who sends the unit in late can ask for the charge to be returned; the Device Service desk decides, not Tier 1.

Advance exchange is not offered on a paid repair, on accessories, or to a customer who already has an advance-exchange hold open.

The replacement under an advance exchange travels by Expedited delivery at our cost. The label for the faulty unit is in the same box.

## 5. Sending the unit in

Return Authorisation. Each claim has a Return Authorisation number beginning RA- and a shipping label, both e-mailed to the customer. The number and the label stay valid for 25 calendar days from the day of issue. After that Device Diagnostics has to issue them again.

What to send. The camera only. The customer keeps the stand or mount, the power adapter and the cables; accessories sent in with a camera are not returned. Any sturdy box will do; the original packaging is not needed.

Where it goes. Units travel under the label to the service centre in Marrow Creek. Under a claim inside the warranty the label is prepaid.

Proof of purchase. For a web store order, the order number is enough. For a camera bought from a retail partner, the customer supplies the dated receipt; warranty service is the same wherever the camera was bought. Where there is neither, the manufacturing date held against the unit is used as the purchase date.

Progress e-mails. The customer is e-mailed at three points: when the unit is scanned in, when the bench result is recorded, and when the outbound parcel is dispatched.

## 6. What counts as a defect

A defect is a failure of materials or workmanship in normal use. The fault code and the bench inspection decide; the customer's description does not.

The following are not defects and go down the paid repair route, whatever the age of the camera:

- impact damage, such as a cracked housing, a cracked lens or a bent stand joint;
- liquid inside a Lantern Cam, or inside an Eave Cam or Trek Cam whose housing has been opened;
- damage caused by a power adapter or charger that is not a Brindlewatch one;
- a unit that has been opened or modified;
- cosmetic marks that do not affect how the camera works.

Weather. Eave Cam and Trek Cam are built to be used outdoors, and water found inside a housing that has never been opened is a defect. Lantern Cam is an indoor camera: fitting one outdoors, or in a bathroom or greenhouse, is not normal use.

More than one fault. Where the bench finds a defect and damage on the same unit, the damage decides the route.

## 7. Paid repair

| Camera | Flat fee |
|---|---|
| Lantern Cam (either generation) | $34 |
| Eave Cam | $52 |
| Trek Cam | $68 |

The flat fee is charged for each unit and is the same whatever the fault. It covers the inbound label, the bench work, the parts and the return of the unit by Standard delivery. If a unit cannot be repaired, a renewed unit of the same model is supplied for the same fee.

Faster return. A customer who wants a repaired unit back sooner can pay the Expedited charge set out in the Shipping and Delivery Policy. It is charged once for each return parcel, not for each unit, and units on the same Return Authorisation travel back in one parcel. Next Business Day is not offered for service returns.

The quote. After the bench inspection the customer is e-mailed a quote. No work is done until the quote has been accepted and paid. The quote stays open for 10 calendar days from the day it is sent. If it has not been accepted in that time, the unit is sent back unrepaired by Standard delivery at no charge.

Turnaround. A repaired unit is dispatched no later than 9 business days after payment. This is a target for dispatch, not a delivery date.

Payment. The fee is paid by card or digital wallet. Account credit cannot be put towards a repair fee.

Several units. Each unit on a Return Authorisation gets its own line on the quote. The customer can accept some lines and decline others; declined units come back unrepaired with the repaired ones.

## 8. Accessories

Accessories sold under the Brindlewatch name - mounts, stands, power adapters, charging cables and weather hoods - are covered against defects for 1 year from purchase. A faulty accessory is exchanged, never repaired, and the exchange is sent by Standard delivery without the old accessory having to come back. Accessories from other makers are not covered, and neither is anything they damage.

An accessory claim does not need a fault code. A Tier 1 agent raises it once the order or receipt has been seen, and the console allows 2 accessory exchanges against any one order before it asks for a Team Lead to sign off the next.

## 9. Units we cannot take in

The service centre does not open a parcel that arrives without a Return Authorisation number on the label; it goes back to the sender. Units sent from outside the United States and Canada are not accepted. A unit that arrives wet, or in packaging that is leaking, is logged by the receiving clerk, sealed and held while Device Diagnostics contacts the customer.

## 10. Customers in Canada

The routes and fees are the same, with three differences.

- The label is not prepaid. The customer pays the postage to Marrow Creek and is reimbursed against a postal receipt, up to $28 for each claim.
- Advance exchange is offered for Lantern Cam and Eave Cam only.
- Outbound parcels are sent duty paid, and the dispatch targets in sections 3 and 7 apply unchanged; delivery then takes the Canada time given in the Shipping and Delivery Policy.

## 11. After the bench inspection

The bench records one of four outcomes.

Fault confirmed. The claim continues down the route already chosen.

No fault found. The unit is sent back as it came. The first no-fault-found return of a camera is free. A second no-fault-found return of the same camera inside one year carries a $19 handling charge, which is taken before the unit is sent back.

Customer damage found on a claim inside the warranty. The claim moves to the paid repair route and a quote is issued. The inspection report, with the technician's images of the damage, is e-mailed to the customer. A customer who disagrees with the report may ask for a second inspection, and has to ask within 21 calendar days of the date on the report. The second inspection is carried out by a different technician, the quote is paused while it is pending, and its result is final. Each claim can have one second inspection.

Repeat fault. When a camera has already been exchanged twice for the same fault code, the next unit sent is new, not renewed, and the Head of Device Service reviews the case before it is dispatched.

## 12. Cover on repair work

A paid repair is covered for 180 days from the day the repaired unit is delivered. If the same fault comes back in that time, we repair the unit again at no charge, with the label prepaid in both directions. The cover applies to the fault that was repaired and to nothing else, and it neither restarts nor lengthens the limited warranty.

A unit supplied in place of one that could not be repaired (section 7) carries the same 180 days.

## 13. What agents say, and what they leave unsaid

- Say that Device Diagnostics decides whether there is a hardware fault and which route applies. Do not tell a customer that a claim, an exchange or an advance exchange will be granted.
- Quote a fee from section 7 only when the warranty position is already recorded; otherwise say that the fee applies if the camera turns out to be outside the warranty or damaged.
- Give the dispatch targets as targets. Do not turn them into a delivery day.
- Do not say whether a replacement unit will be new or renewed.
- What happens to subscription charges while a camera is away is a matter for the Refund and Cancellation Policy, not for these terms.
- A customer who is unhappy with a bench outcome is told about the second inspection where it applies. Do not offer a fee waiver; only the Device Service desk can waive a fee.

## 14. The Device Service desk

The Device Service desk is the team at the service centre that owns claims once a unit has been scanned in. Tier 1 reaches it through the claim record in the support console, by adding a note marked for the desk; the desk answers notes by the end of the next business day. Customers are not put through to the desk, and all contact with the customer about a claim goes through Customer Care or the progress e-mails.

## 15. Records

Each claim record carries the fault code, the Return Authorisation number, the route, the proof of purchase used, the bench outcome and, for a paid repair, the dates on which the quote was sent, accepted and paid. Agents add what the customer was told on each contact.
