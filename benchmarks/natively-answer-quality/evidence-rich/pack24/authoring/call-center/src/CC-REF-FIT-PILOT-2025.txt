BRINDLEWATCH FIT - PILOT BRIEFING FOR CUSTOMER CARE

Issued 10 November 2025
From: Wilhelmina Castellanos-Reid, Fit Pilot Lead
To: all Tier 1 agents and Team Leads
Pilot period: 24 November 2025 to 31 March 2026


WHAT IS HAPPENING

From 24 November we are trying out a home installation service under the name Brindlewatch Fit. An installer visits the customer, puts the cameras up and makes sure they are working before leaving. Customers have been asking for this since the Eave Cam launched, and the pilot is how we find out whether we can offer it at a sensible price and to a standard we are happy with.

The pilot is small on purpose. Please read the limits below before you offer it to anyone.


WHERE

Two areas only: Harrowmere and Dunmere Heights. The console will not let you book an address anywhere else. If a customer outside those two areas asks, say that the service is being tried in a small number of places and is not available where they live yet. Do not suggest when it might be.


WHO DOES THE WORK

One partner, Thornapple Home Services. Their installers have been trained on Lantern Cam and Eave Cam at our offices. They carry a Thornapple identity card; there is no Brindlewatch badge during the pilot.


PILOT PRICES

  First camera in a visit ............... $69
  Every additional camera, same visit ... $39

A visit covers up to 4 cameras. There is no high-mount work in the pilot: installers will not fix anything more than 10 feet above the ground, and there is no travel fee because both areas are close to the Thornapple depots.

The full amount is taken when you book.

Please do not describe the pilot prices as an introductory offer or suggest that they will last. They were set to find out what customers will pay, and they may go up or down.


BOOKING

Book in the Fit calendar (new tab in the console, visible from 24 November). There are two arrival windows during the pilot, a morning one from 8 am to 12 noon and an afternoon one from 12 noon to 5 pm. Visits run on weekdays only.

The customer gets a confirmation by e-mail. Thornapple also telephones the customer on the working day before the visit to confirm.

The cameras must already be at the address when you book. During the pilot we are not booking visits against orders that are still on their way, because a late parcel wastes a crew's half day.


HOW TO BRING IT UP

Offer Fit when a customer in one of the two areas tells you they are not confident putting a camera up, or asks whether we know someone who can. Do not offer it on every contact from those areas, and do not offer it on a contact about a fault or a complaint. One sentence is enough: we are trying out a fitting service where they live, this is what it costs, would they like a visit. If they say no, leave it there.


CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS DURING THE PILOT

The customer can move or cancel a visit free of charge until 6 pm on the day before it. After 6 pm on the day before, or if nobody is in when the installer calls, a $20 late fee is kept and the rest goes back to the customer.

Team Leads can waive the late fee during the pilot where they think it is fair. Agents cannot.


IF THE CUSTOMER SENDS THE CAMERA BACK

During the pilot, when a camera fitted by us is returned inside the normal return period, the entire Fit charge for that camera goes back to the customer along with the camera's price. We will look at this again once we know how often it happens.


GUARANTEE

Thornapple guarantee their mounting work for 6 months. If a customer reports a loose mount in that time, book a return visit in the calendar under the label REFIX; there is no charge and the visit does not count towards the pilot numbers.


WHAT THE INSTALLER WILL AND WILL NOT DO

Will: fix the mount, connect the camera on the customer's phone, check the view with the customer.

Will not: touch house wiring, go on a roof, fit other makes of camera, or deal with anything to do with the customer's plan or bill. Please make that last point clear when you book, because the installers have been getting asked.


WHAT WE ARE MEASURING

- Visits completed: the aim is 400 across the two areas by the end of March.
- Share of visits finished inside the arrival window.
- Customers' rating of the visit, from a short survey e-mailed the next morning.
- How many cameras fitted under Fit come back, compared with cameras customers fit themselves.

You do not need to collect any of this. It comes from the calendar and the survey.


WHAT TO NOTE ON THE CONTACT

Put FIT PILOT at the start of your notes on any contact where the service was offered, whether or not the customer booked. It is how we count interest.


WHERE TO SEND THINGS

Anything the calendar will not let you do, any complaint about a visit, and any request from a customer outside the two areas who is insistent: the pilot mailbox, fitpilot@brindlewatch.example. It is read on weekdays and you should have an answer the same day.


AFTER THE PILOT

The pilot ends on 31 March 2026. Prices, the number of cameras in a visit, the cancellation rule and the areas will all be looked at again before anything is offered more widely, and nothing in this briefing should be read as what the service will look like afterwards. A fuller guide will take the place of this briefing if the service continues.
