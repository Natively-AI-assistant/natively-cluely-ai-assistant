# Brindlewatch Privacy and Camera Access Policy

Version 5.0 | Effective 1 March 2026
Policy owner: Thaddeus Okonkwo-Reyes, Privacy Officer | Signed off by the Executive Committee on 18 February 2026

## 1. The principle

The cameras are the customer's eyes, not ours. Brindlewatch runs the service that carries and stores video; it does not watch it. This policy sets out exactly who can see what, and Customer Care agents should be able to explain it plainly when a customer asks.

## 2. Live view

No Brindlewatch employee or contractor, at any tier, can watch a customer's live view. There is no internal tool that opens a live stream. The stream is encrypted between the camera and the devices signed in to the customer's account.

## 3. Recordings

Recorded clips are stored encrypted. Tier 1 agents cannot open, play or download a clip, and the support console shows no video, no still images and no audio.

There are only two situations in which a member of staff can see a recording:

1. The customer shares it. Using Share with Support in the app, the customer picks one clip. That single clip can then be viewed for 72 hours, and only by the Tier 2 Device Diagnostics engineer assigned to the ticket. Every viewing is logged, and the customer can withdraw the share in the app at any moment.
2. The law requires it. See section 6.

What agents do see: whether each camera is online, when it was last online, its firmware version, the plan on the account and how many motion events were recorded each day.

## 4. How long clips are kept

| Plan | Clips kept for |
|---|---|
| Keep Basic | 14 days |
| Keep Plus | 30 days |
| Keep Pro | 60 days |

After that a clip is deleted automatically. Cancelling a plan does not delete the clips already recorded; each one stays until its own retention period runs out. Customers can download any clip to their phone before then.

## 5. Deleting recordings

Customers delete their own recordings. In the app: Settings > Privacy > Delete all recordings, or single clips from the timeline. The app asks for the account password first.

What happens then: the clips disappear from the account immediately, are erased from primary storage within 24 hours and from backups within 30 days. Deletion cannot be undone.

Agents cannot delete recordings from the support console. If the customer cannot use the app, the Tier 1 agent completes step-up verification and files a Deletion Request (form DR-2). The Privacy Office carries it out within 4 business days and confirms by e-mail.

Erasing an entire account - all personal data, not only video - is a separate request to the Privacy Office and is completed within 30 calendar days.

Recordings under a legal preservation order are held until the order ends. Tier 1 agents are not told which accounts these are, and refer such enquiries to the Privacy Office.

## 6. Police, courts and lawyers

Every request from police, a government body, a court or a lawyer is handled by the Legal Response team and by nobody else: legalresponse@brindlewatch.example. A Tier 1 agent who receives such a call does not confirm whether the person named has an account, shares nothing, gives the Legal Response address and logs the call.

Recordings are handed over only in response to a search warrant or court order. A subpoena obtains basic account details only, never video.

Emergencies. Where there is imminent danger of death or serious physical injury, Legal Response may disclose without a court order. Only Legal Response makes that decision; the team can be reached around the clock.

We tell the account holder before disclosing, unless the law forbids it.

A customer who wants the police to have their own footage can download the clips and hand them over themselves.

## 7. Other commitments

- Sound recording is off by default on Eave Cam and can be switched on by the customer.
- Familiar Faces, which recognises regular visitors, is opt-in; the customer can erase its data at any time.
- We do not sell customer data or video.
- Everyone at Brindlewatch with access to customer systems completes privacy training every year.

## 8. Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced to customers by e-mail at least 30 days before they take effect. Version 5.0 added Share with Support and set out the deletion timings in section 5.
