I've mentored maybe 15 to 20 engineers over the last seven or eight years, mostly senior and staff level, and I'd say a solid 8 out of 10 there. My focus has been on incident leadership, technical depth across distributed systems, and helping people grow into more senior IC roles, mostly through pairing during real incidents rather than formal sessions.
