Limited info on the Hadoop migration specifics from what's loaded, going off what's public:

The hard part was almost always the semantics, not the transport. Batch jobs let you get away with late-arriving data, small windows of inconsistency, and reorderings across partition boundaries. Streaming forces you to define an event time, a watermark strategy, and an out-of-order tolerance up front, so anything the batch pipeline was implicitly glossing over has to be made explicit. Idempotency on sinks, exactly-once versus effectively-once, schema evolution on Avro, and backfilling historical partitions without breaking downstream consumers were the real failure modes we had to design around.
</answer>
