--- Raw model answer ---
I ran a 1.2k-node production cluster with the control plane spread across three AZs, etcd encrypted at rest, and Istio mesh on Envoy for east-west traffic. Biggest operational learnings were around node churn during upgrades, so we leaned hard on PodDisruptionBudgets, surge upgrades, and PDB-aware Cluster Autoscaler to keep rollouts safe. HPA on custom Prometheus metrics for latency-sensitive services, plus KEDA for queue-driven scale-out on the async workers.
