The paper's main contribution is the Transformer, a new sequence transduction architecture built entirely on attention and dispensing with recurrence and convolutions. The Transformer relies on **multi-head self-attention** to draw global dependencies between all positions in constant time, along with positional encodings to retain order information. The authors show this design achieves state-of-the-art quality on machine translation while being **more parallelizable and faster to train** than RNN- or convolution-based encoders.
